Who We Are

We are a group of working professionals collaborating to prepare and compile Veda books on specific subjects. We are geographically distributed across many locations, united by our dedication to helping Veda students.

We focus exclusively on Krishna Yajur Veda mantras, providing materials and articles that assist learners at various stages of their Vedic studies. We do not cover karma kanda or procedures - only the mantras and their learning materials.
